About this property

Welcome to 66 Bear Trace in the desirable Palmer Woods subdivision! This like-new 3BR/2BA brick home offers one-level living with a clean, modern feel and thoughtful upgrades throughout. Built in 2022 by Kitzman Construction, the home features an open floor plan anchored by a spacious great room with a vaulted ceiling. From there, the layout flows seamlessly into the breakfast nook and kitchen on one side, and into the formal dining room on the other-perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. Enjoy durable LVP flooring in the main living areas and cozy carpet in the bedrooms, with ceramic tile in the bathrooms and laundry room. The kitchen is a standout with shaker-style cabinetry, granite countertops, stainless steel appliances (including the refrigerator), and plenty of workspace. The primary suite offers a tray ceiling, double vanity, walk-in shower, and a large walk-in closet with custom wood shelving. Both bathrooms feature granite countertops for a consistent, upscale finish. The laundry room is as functional as it is stylish with ceramic tile flooring, storage cabinets, and a custom built-in bench with cubbies and hooks. Situated on a level lot with a 2-car attached garage, this home combines low-maintenance living with modern design in a convenient Jackson location.
