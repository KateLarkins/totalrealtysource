# Total Realty Source Search Visibility Benchmark

Checked: August 24, 2026

## Current first-two-page visibility

The following searches were checked in Google with personalization disabled where supported. Total Realty Source did not appear within the first 20 organic results:

- homes for sale in Jackson TN
- 5 acres for sale West Tennessee
- new homes in Jackson TN
- homes for sale in Humboldt TN
- homes for rent in Jackson TN
- land for sale in West Tennessee
- homes for sale in Medina TN
- West Tennessee real estate agents

The representative Safari search for “homes for sale in Jackson TN” produced the same Google result set and did not include Total Realty Source on the first page. Safari is a browser; its results depend on the search engine selected in Safari.

## Indexing diagnosis

Google currently indexes the homepage, Contact, About, and the general For Sale page. The newer city, specialty, and permanent listing pages are live and crawlable but were not yet visible in Google's indexed result set during this check.

## Fixes applied

- City “Homes for Sale” pages now contain residential homes only, excluding land, commercial properties, and rentals.
- Jackson, Humboldt, and Medina pages now include unique local search copy.
- City pages now include `CollectionPage`, `ItemList`, and breadcrumb structured data.
- Added dedicated landing pages for West Tennessee land and acreage and West Tennessee homes for rent.
- Added prominent internal links to the high-intent pages from the homepage, For Sale page, and generated-page footers.
- Added Open Graph metadata to city and regional intent pages.
- Removed the crawl block for the obsolete closed-listings page and added `noindex,follow` to that page so Google can process its removal.
- Regenerated and validated the page, listing, and image XML sitemaps.

## Required recheck

Rankings cannot update until these changes are published and Google recrawls them. After deployment, submit `https://totalrealtysource.com/sitemap_index.xml` in Google Search Console and request indexing for the most important landing pages. Recheck the same query set after Google reports the pages as indexed.
